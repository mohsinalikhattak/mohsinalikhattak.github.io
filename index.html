About

Software QA Engineer focused on test automation and quality strategy for web and mobile applications. At AntonX, I've contributed to six projects, reducing overall testing time by 30% through automation and improving test script performance by 29% by refining Selenium and Playwright suites — building teams' confidence to ship faster without trading away quality.

I find genuine satisfaction in catching what others miss, the edge case that breaks checkout, the API response that silently drops a field, the regression that only shows up under load. My work spans the full test lifecycle, from writing clear, comprehensive test plans and test cases through to release, not just automation on top.

Core Expertise:

  • Writing clear, comprehensive test plans and test cases from requirements.
  • Designing and maintaining automation scripts with Selenium, Cypress, Playwright and Appium.
  • API testing with Postman, and cross-browser testing with BrowserStack.
  • Load and performance testing with JMeter and BlazeMeter.
  • SQL-based data validation and analyzing test results for database, usability and functional issues.
  • Full command of SDLC, STLC and the bug lifecycle, applied across Agile/Scrum and Waterfall projects.

I care as much about why a defect happened as I do about finding it, root-cause thinking is what turns a bug report into something a development team can actually act on.

Tools:
Automation: Selenium, Cypress, Playwright, Appium, WebdriverIO.
Testing & Compatibility: BrowserStack, Postman.
Performance: JMeter, BlazeMeter, K6
Documentation & Tracking: Jira, Confluence.

My project work has spanned point-of-sale and retail platforms, real estate and government-integrated systems, social networking applications, and marketplace products — each with its own quality demands, from payment reconciliation to cross-system data consistency.

I believe good QA isn't a gate at the end of a sprint — it's a habit built into how a team ships software. I work closely with developers and product owners from requirements through release, because the earlier quality is part of the conversation, the less it costs to fix.
